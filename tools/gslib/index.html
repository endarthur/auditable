Decluster

Preferentially-sampled data lies about its mean. declus sweeps cell sizes and weights samples by how crowded their cell is.

Variography

gamv — the same pair-counting, the same accumulation order as the Fortran. Directions take a dip for 3D data; with 2D data leave dip 0.

Model

Nugget + nested structures — vmodel, the exact covariance the kriging will use, redrawn over the experimental points below on every edit.

Kriging

kb2d (2D) / kt3d (3D). The result lands in the viewer as a block model — switch estimate / variance up top.

Simulation

nscore → sgsim → backtr: the data goes to normal scores (declustering weights applied if you ran declus), one realization is simulated in Gaussian space on the kriging grid, and back-transformed through the score table. The model is your fitted one rescaled to unit sill — proper practice fits the normal-scores variogram itself; this is the teaching shortcut, and it is labelled as one.
